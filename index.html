Frequently asked questions

Your Questions, Answered

Ask Us Directly
Do you provide free estimates?

Yes. Request a free quote using the form above. We’ll follow up by phone, text or email to discuss your project and arrange your home consultation.

Do you measure the windows?

Yes. We measure your windows for free during your home consultation so your blinds can be planned for the specific windows.

Do you install the blinds?

Yes. BlindsFold is being built around custom blind supply and professional installation in the Winnipeg area.

What areas of Winnipeg do you serve?

Winnipeg and surrounding areas are the initial service market. Send your postal code or area in the quote form and we’ll confirm availability.

How long does an order usually take?

Timelines depend on the selected style, availability and project size. We’ll give you the expected timing before you proceed.

Do you offer blackout blinds?

Blackout and room-darkening options are part of the initial product range. Exact fabrics and configurations are confirmed during consultation.

Do you offer motorized blinds?

Motorized blinds are included in the planned range. We’ll confirm available operating options for your specific windows.

Can I send pictures of my windows first?

Yes. The quote form is designed to support an optional photo once file upload is enabled, and you can also text photos to 431-788-5688.
