What matters to us

Professional Without the Noise

Version 1 of BlindsFold is intentionally focused. The website helps Winnipeg homeowners explore styles, send project details and start a conversation. Pricing, measurement requirements, material availability and timelines are then confirmed directly.

We are not presenting stock imagery as completed client work, inventing customer reviews, or making unsupported “#1” claims. As real installations and feedback grow, the website will grow with them.

Winnipeg-focused

The initial market is Winnipeg and surrounding areas.

Lead-first process

Built to evolve

Product categories, gallery photos and customer reviews can expand without redesigning the brand.
