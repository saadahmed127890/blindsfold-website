Privacy Policy

Last updated November 12, 2026. This policy explains how BlindsFold handles personal information in connection with blindsfold.com and requests for window blinds, consultations, measurements, estimates and installation in Winnipeg and surrounding areas, Manitoba, Canada.

1. Information collected

Our quote form asks for your name, phone number, email address, preferred blind style, approximate number of windows, and postal code or project area. You may optionally attach a window or room photo. The form also identifies the request as coming from our website. Please avoid including people, identification documents or other sensitive information in photos.

When you contact us by phone, text or email, you choose what information to provide. Further details, such as an installation address and measurements, may be needed to arrange the services you request. The website does not collect payments.

2. Why we use your information

Quote details help us understand your project and service area, respond to questions, discuss blind options, arrange measurements, prepare estimates, and coordinate requested installation. Advertising tracking is optional and separate from handling your request.

3. How quote requests are processed

The website sends your completed form, including an optional attached photo, directly to Formspree over HTTPS. Formspree processes submissions for BlindsFold. Photo delivery depends on the enabled service plan. The success message means the form service accepted the request; it is not a guarantee that an email has reached our inbox. If you need to follow up, contact info@blindsfold.com.

Submitting a quote asks us to contact you about that request. The website does not include a promotional mailing-list subscription in the quote submission.

4. Cookies, Meta Pixel and advertising

When enabled and with your permission, Meta Pixel measures visits and successful quote submissions from our Facebook and Instagram advertising and can support relevant advertising. The Pixel may send Meta browser and device information, IP address, cookie identifiers, the page URL and referrer, and the PageView or Lead event. Meta may associate this activity with your Meta account and activity on other websites.

Our Pixel integration does not include your name, email, phone number, postal code, quote answers or photos in event parameters. It does not enable advanced matching or a server-side Conversions API. Meta’s handling of information is described in its Privacy Policy.

5. Your advertising choices

Meta’s advertising script does not load unless you select “Accept advertising.” Selecting “Decline advertising” leaves it off, and you can still use the quote form. Use , also available in the footer, to change your choice.

We store this preference in your browser’s local storage for up to 180 days. Clearing browser data, switching browsers or using another device may require a new choice. If storage is unavailable, the choice applies only to the current page. Withdrawing consent stops future Pixel events from this website and attempts to remove accessible first-party Meta cookies. It does not undo information already sent to Meta or remove cookies controlled by other websites.

6. Service providers and information sharing

Formspree processes the quote information submitted through this website. Cloudflare is used in delivering the live website and forwarding messages addressed to our business email to a Gmail inbox. Google provides that email service. These services may process technical connection information to operate their services. See Formspree’s Privacy Policy and Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy, and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Meta receives advertising information only as described above. Information may also need to be disclosed where required by applicable law. Contact us about the providers involved in your particular request.

7. Processing outside Canada

Formspree states that it uses technical infrastructure in the United States and may process information in other countries where it operates. Other service providers, including Meta when advertising tracking is accepted, may process information outside Canada. Information processed abroad may be subject to foreign laws and lawful access by authorities.

8. Protection and security

The live website and configured form endpoint use HTTPS to protect information in transit. Access to quote information is limited to the people who need it to respond to your request and arrange your project, together with the service providers described above. No online transmission or storage system is completely secure. Please do not send payment-card details, identity documents or sensitive personal information through the quote form. Contact us if you have a concern about information you have submitted.

9. Retention and deletion

Quote information may be held in the form service, email correspondence and records used to handle your project. We retain it only as long as needed to handle your inquiry, provide requested services, and meet applicable legal obligations. We review records and delete or anonymize information that is no longer needed. Contact info@blindsfold.com to ask about the records we hold and request deletion. Some records may need to be retained to meet legal obligations or resolve outstanding matters. There is no single retention period for every type of record.

10. Access, correction and withdrawal of consent

You may contact us to request access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate information, or withdrawal of consent, subject to applicable legal or contractual restrictions. We may need to verify your identity before disclosing or changing records. Withdrawing information needed to provide a requested service may affect our ability to complete it. Advertising consent can be withdrawn independently using the controls above.

11. Marketing communications

A quote request is not a subscription to unrelated promotional messages. Any separate commercial electronic marketing must follow applicable requirements under Canada’s Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL), including consent where required, sender identification and an unsubscribe method. Requested replies and estimates have distinct rules under CASL. You can tell us if you no longer want follow-up about your quote.

12. Canadian privacy framework

The Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) generally governs personal information handled by private businesses in commercial activities in Manitoba. Manitoba’s public-sector Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy Act (FIPPA) does not generally apply to a private blinds business. Manitoba’s Privacy Act provides separate protection against certain invasions of privacy. This policy is not a certification or guarantee of legal compliance.

13. Policy updates

When this policy changes, the updated version and date will appear on this page. Material changes to advertising purposes or information sharing should be explained before new consent is requested where required.

14. Privacy questions or concerns

Contact BlindsFold at info@blindsfold.com with “Privacy inquiry” in the subject line. Our service area is Winnipeg and surrounding areas, Manitoba, Canada. You can also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada about your privacy rights or concerns.
